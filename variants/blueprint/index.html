SHEET 01/03

General Notes

Agent development is undergoing a crucial shift. Despite remarkable advances in agents that reason, plan, and operate in open-ended environments, their construction remains fragmented, dominated by manual prompt engineering and ad-hoc scaffolding.

The bottleneck is not a deficit of agentic frameworks but verification: the ability to reliably determine whether a change to an agent is a genuine improvement. This workshop convenes researchers from reinforcement learning, agentic AI, evaluation, AI safety, simulation, and ML systems to formalize verification as a core discipline.

SHEET 02/03

Specifications · Three Pillars

SPEC-01

Safety & Robustness of Verification

Verifiers that prevent reward hacking, resist specification gaming, and scale from outcome checks to process-level signals: traces, plans, tool-call sequences.

SPEC-02

Environment-Grounded Verification & Simulators

Where closed-form metrics do not exist, the environment is the ground truth. Faithful simulators become a verification infrastructure layer.

SPEC-03

Diverse & Heterogeneous Verifiable Signals

User experience, cost/latency profiles, calibration, and multimodality — composed into reliable verification metrics.
