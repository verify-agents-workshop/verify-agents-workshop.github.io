About the Workshop

Agent development is undergoing a crucial shift. Despite remarkable advances in agents that reason, plan, and operate in open-ended environments, their construction remains fragmented — dominated by manual prompt engineering and ad-hoc scaffolding.

The bottleneck is not a deficit of agentic frameworks but verification: the ability to reliably determine whether a change to an agent is a genuine improvement. This workshop seeks to formalize verification as a core discipline in agent development.

Three Pillars of Verification

Exhibit I

Safety & Robustness of Verification

Process-level signals on agent traces, 2026

Robust verifiers that prevent reward hacking, resist specification gaming, and scale from outcome checks to traces, plans, and tool-call sequences.

Exhibit II

Environment-Grounded Verification & Simulators

Faithful simulation, ground truth, 2026

Where closed-form metrics do not exist, the environment itself is the ground truth — simulators as a verification infrastructure layer.

Exhibit III

Diverse & Heterogeneous Verifiable Signals

Composite metrics, mixed media, 2026

User experience, cost and latency, calibration, and multimodality — heterogeneous signals composed into reliable verification metrics.
